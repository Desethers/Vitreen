## X — simple posts

### 1. Positionnement

Most galleries do not need another heavy platform.

They need their existing artwork data to circulate better:

from inventory,
to private selections,
to PDFs,
to collector conversations.

That is the layer we are building with Vitreen.

### 2. Artwork Library

An artwork file should not stay locked in an inventory.

It should be ready to become:

a viewing room,
a private PDF,
a collector email,
a WhatsApp follow-up,
a page for an exhibition.

The archive is only useful when it can move.

### 3. Sales Continuity

The problem is not that galleries lack tools.

It is that the conversation around an artwork often gets separated from the artwork itself.

Inquiry in email.
PDF in a folder.
Notes in WhatsApp.
Availability somewhere else.

Vitreen connects that sales continuity.

### 4. Anti-platform

We are not trying to replace how galleries work.

Most galleries already have habits, files, contacts, emails, archives and internal rituals.

Vitreen sits around that environment and makes artwork circulation easier.

Less migration.
More movement.

## X — thread

### The real workflow behind one collector selection

**Tweet 1**
One collector asks for a selection.

What looks like one email often becomes a full internal workflow for the gallery.

This is where most digital tools still miss the reality of the job.

**Tweet 2**
First, someone has to find the right works.

Not only available works.
Works that make sense for the collector, the artist, the current exhibition, the price range and the relationship.

**Tweet 3**
Then the artwork data has to be checked.

Images.
Dimensions.
Medium.
Price.
Availability.
Provenance.
Installation shots.
Previous exhibition context.

This information is rarely in one clean place.

**Tweet 4**
Then the gallery prepares the material.

Sometimes a PDF.
Sometimes a viewing room.
Sometimes a short email.
Sometimes a WhatsApp message with one image and a note.

The format changes, but the artwork source should stay consistent.

**Tweet 5**
Then comes the follow-up.

Did the collector open it?
Which work did they ask about?
Was availability confirmed?
Who on the team is replying?

This is where sales continuity usually breaks.

**Tweet 6**
Vitreen is built around this simple idea:

one artwork source should feed the materials galleries already use to sell.

Private selections.
PDFs.
Viewing rooms.
Collector follow-up.

Not a heavier platform. A cleaner circulation layer.
